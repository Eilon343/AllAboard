# **Pitch: All Aboard\!**

## **Core loop**

Steer **:** Guide the ship using one thumb while adapting to wind and environmental physics. Encounter **:** Intercept passing vessels and make split-second choices: Attack (automatically) to plunder resources, or Befriend to build alliances.

Reward **:** Collect immediate points and loot, fueling the upgrade loop and keeping the momentum going.

## **Meta**

Boat Upgrades: Enhance core ship stats such as HP, attack power, and speed. Dynamic Diplomacy: Build foreign relations that directly influence your success when befriending other vessels. Customization & Boosters: Unlock cosmetic skins and powerful temporary buffs. Daily Retention & Engagement: Earn rewards through daily tasks, login bonuses, and streak prizes.

## **Why come back?**

Daily tasks, login streaks, and recurring rewards.

Progression milestones, daily streaks, and the drive to fully upgrade your ship. Simple and fun, time killer.

Quick sessions: Simple controls, instant loading, and an easy learning curve.

## **Who and when**

Target audience: Casual players looking for a quick, engaging time-killer. Occasions: At the bus station, between classes, or waiting in line. Session design: Short, highly addictive, 1-3 minute gameplay loops.

## **Feel**

Adaptive audio loop that shifts dynamically based on the situation (friendly/aggressive).

Rewarding sound that will play when you do something good (which will happen a lot). In terms of motion, arcady responsive fluid movement.

Haptics will be triggered by getting attacked or attacking other ships or by getting rewards/notifications. Nice effects when destroying a ship or befriending one.

Weather particles and effects.

Numbers when attacking a ship, will float on the other ship with nice effects that make you feel strong.

## **Scope check**

- ☑ Hybrid-casual: simple core \+ a meta layer

- ☑ Portrait, one thumb

- ☑ 1–3 minute sessions
